KhabarHub

News platform with AI rewriting and automatic social posting

The problem

A small news team spent more time reformatting and re-posting stories than reporting them.

What I built

A Laravel news platform where editors publish once: an LLM rewrites and summarises stories into house style, and each article is auto-posted to the team's social channels.

The outcome

Publishing went from a chain of copy-paste steps to one button.

Laravel · MySQL · LLM rewriting · Social auto-posting
