University Admission System

Full admission pipeline for CUVAS, from application to merit list

The problem

Admissions ran on paper forms, bank queues and manual merit calculations. Applicants had no visibility, and staff re-entered the same data at every stage.

What I built

Three roles. Students register with OTP (email and WhatsApp), save drafts, upload documents and print their form and fee challan. Admins manage cycles, departments and programmes, verify applications in bulk, record entry tests with an automatic re-test loop and publish merit lists. Department heads get read-only oversight, and every report exports to Excel.

The outcome

The whole cycle, from application to enrolment, runs through one system, and applicants track their own status instead of calling the office.

Laravel 12 · PHP 8.2 · MySQL · PDF · Excel exports · OTP via email & WhatsApp
