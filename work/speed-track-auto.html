Speed Track Auto

Bilingual Laravel site for a Saudi car wash chain, rebuilt from WordPress

The problem

The old WordPress site was a static brochure that couldn't present packages, loyalty or branches in both Arabic and English.

What I built

A ground-up Laravel rebuild: fully bilingual with proper RTL, monthly wash subscriptions, the five-visit loyalty programme, corporate vouchers and branch pages for Makkah and Jeddah, with WhatsApp contact built in.

The outcome

Live at speedtrackauto.com, connected to the systems that run the business.

Laravel · MySQL · EN/AR · RTL · WhatsApp
