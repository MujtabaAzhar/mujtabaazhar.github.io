Smart Tracker

ESP32 hardware wired into car wash PLCs, syncing every wash to the cloud

The problem

Owners had no independent record of how many washes their machines performed. Counts lived inside the PLC: invisible, unauditable and easy to dispute.

What I built

A custom ESP32 device wired to the PLC of each machine. It listens for wash signals on the wire, logs each wash to a central PHP/MySQL server, and keeps working through outages: events are stored locally and sync the moment the connection returns.

The outcome

Every wash across every branch is counted independently of the machine, with zero data loss during network drops. Hardware, firmware and server, built by one person.

ESP32 · Arduino · C++ · PHP · MySQL · Offline-first sync
