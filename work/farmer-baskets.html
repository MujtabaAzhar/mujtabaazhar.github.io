Farmer's Basket

Mango e-commerce and POS shipping orchard-fresh fruit across Pakistan

The problem

Mango season is short and intense. Sales ran on phone calls and walk-ins, with no online ordering, no live stock by variety, and counter sales in a separate ledger.

What I built

A storefront and point of sale in one system: a variety catalogue (Chounsa, Sindhri, Anwar Ratol) with live stock, cart and checkout, accounts, wishlists, real-time order and shipment tracking, and an integrated POS drawing from the same inventory. Plus a full SEO and performance audit.

The outcome

Live at farmerbaskets.com. Online and in-person sales share one stock count, so numbers stay true through the peak-season rush.

Laravel · PHP 8 · MySQL · JavaScript · POS · Order tracking
