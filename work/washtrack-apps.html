WashTrack Mobile Apps

Two apps from one Flutter codebase: a loyalty card in your pocket, and a fast counter app

The problem

Customers lost paper stamp cards, and counter staff typed plates and phone numbers by hand at a busy lane.

What I built

One Flutter codebase with two flavours. WashTrack (customers): sign in with a Saudi mobile and a WhatsApp code, an offline QR loyalty card, points and tiers with progress to the next free wash, wash packages, visit history with VAT in SAR, offers, ratings and problem reports with photos. WashTrack Business (staff and fleets): scan the card QR or search by Saudi plate or phone, record a wash with a live total, role-based alerts and history, and corporate fleets issuing wash vouchers to drivers as QR codes over WhatsApp.

The outcome

Built for Saudi Arabia from day one: full Arabic RTL and English, plates drawn as they look on the car with Arabic above Latin, phone fields that only accept Saudi mobiles, the new Riyal symbol, and push notifications that open the right screen.

Flutter · Dart · REST API · Push notifications · WhatsApp OTP · EN/AR · RTL · Android & iOS
