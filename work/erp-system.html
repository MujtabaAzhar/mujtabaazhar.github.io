Multi-branch ERP

Multi-branch, multi-language ERP for a foreign enterprise client

The problem

A growing multi-branch business ran on spreadsheets: sales, purchases, stock, staff and orders in disconnected files.

What I built

Daily sales and purchase entry, employee management, WhatsApp notifications, PDF reports, multi-branch support, QR scanning, order tracking, stock control, an evaluation system and multi-language support throughout.

The outcome

Every sale, purchase and stock movement across branches runs through one system, with reports a click away.

Laravel · MySQL · WhatsApp API · PDF · QR scanning · i18n
