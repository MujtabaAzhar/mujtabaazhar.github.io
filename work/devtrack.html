DevTrack

Task and development tracking with an assign → accept → approve workflow and WhatsApp alerts

The problem

Work was assigned in chat messages and tracked in people's heads. Nobody could prove a task was accepted, finished or signed off.

What I built

A cross-department task lifecycle (New, In Progress, Ready for Testing, Completed, On Hold) with attachments, comments, search and filters. Role-based access for admin, manager, developer and user, CSRF protection, soft deletes and a full audit history, with configurable WhatsApp alert triggers and a delivery log. Later ported in full to MongoDB, Express, React and Node, keeping every screen, permission and the bilingual Arabic/English RTL interface.

The outcome

Every task has an owner, a state and a paper trail from assignment to approval, and the WhatsApp alerts chase people so managers don't have to.

PHP 8 · MySQL · MERN port · Bootstrap 5 · Chart.js · WhatsApp alerts · EN/AR · RTL
