UH Builder & Electrician

Company website with its own admin panel and analytics, zero setup

The problem

A construction and electrical company needed a site they could update themselves, on cheap hosting, with no database to configure.

What I built

A PHP 8 + SQLite website and admin panel: upload the folder and it runs. The admin edits content and shows page views, unique and first-time visitors and change versus the previous period. Data and includes are blocked from the web.

The outcome

The owner updates the site and sees its traffic without a developer or a database server.

PHP 8 · SQLite · Admin panel · Visitor analytics
