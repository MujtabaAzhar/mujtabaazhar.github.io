Membership System

Multi-tenant memberships with instant QR activation and Telr payments

The problem

Businesses selling recurring service packages needed to sell, activate and verify memberships across branches without manual bookkeeping.

What I built

A multi-tenant membership platform: dynamic packages, instant QR generation on purchase, branch-level scanning, WhatsApp activation alerts and a full admin panel, with payments through the Telr gateway.

The outcome

One codebase serves several businesses in isolation. A customer buys a package and holds an active, scannable membership within seconds.

Laravel · MySQL · Telr payments · WhatsApp API · Multi-tenant
