Maintenance Workflow System

End-to-end task visibility for maintenance operations

The problem

Maintenance requests disappeared into phone calls and chat threads. Nobody could say what was pending, who owned it or how long it had been sitting.

What I built

A workflow covering the full life of a maintenance order: equipment and sub-components, technician diagnosis, start and complete work, temporary fixes, parts repair, requests for more time or outside support, QR labels and real-time status.

The outcome

Managers see the whole maintenance pipeline at a glance, and tasks stopped falling through the cracks.

Laravel · MySQL · QR codes · Real-time notifications · PDF
