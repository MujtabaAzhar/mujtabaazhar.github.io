Manufacturing & Inventory Tracking

Tracks every part in every machine built, and what each machine really cost

The problem

When the factory built a car wash machine, parts came from the warehouse or were bought directly, and nobody could say who took what or what the machine actually cost.

What I built

Manufacturing orders where every part is traced from source to final status: warehouse parts through request, approval and issue; direct purchases through request, approval, invoice and receiving. Named roles for admin, inventory registration and requesters, moving weighted-average costing, configurable currency and VAT, and a full audit log.

The outcome

The true cost of each manufactured machine is a report, not a guess, and every part has a name against it.

Laravel 12 · MySQL · Tailwind CSS · Alpine.js · Weighted-average costing · Audit log
