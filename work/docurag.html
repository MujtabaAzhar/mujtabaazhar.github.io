DocuRAG

Grounded Q&A over your own documents, with a citation for every claim

The problem

Chatbots over company documents sound confident even when the answer isn't in the documents at all.

What I built

Upload a PDF, DOCX, HTML or TXT and ask in natural language. A restart-safe ingestion pipeline, hybrid retrieval (dense vectors + keyword) fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion and re-ranked with a cross-encoder, then grounded generation that cites every claim and explicitly refuses when the documents don't contain the answer. Streaming chat, a Retrieval Lab to inspect what was found, a scored evaluation harness over a fixed corpus, and per-IP rate limiting.

The outcome

Answers you can check, measured by an evaluation suite instead of by feel.

Python · FastAPI · Qdrant · PostgreSQL · Redis · Groq · React · SSE streaming · Docker
