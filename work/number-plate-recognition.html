Number Plate Recognition

Point a phone camera at a car and the plate is read and ready

The problem

Identifying a returning car meant typing its plate by hand: slow at a busy branch, and one typo breaks the customer's history.

What I built

A camera-based plate reader on an ordinary phone. Staff snap the car; the system extracts the plate's letters and numbers and hands them to visit logging, loyalty lookup and membership checks.

The outcome

A car is identified in the time it takes to take a photo, and plate entry errors disappear.

Mobile camera · OCR · Laravel · REST API
