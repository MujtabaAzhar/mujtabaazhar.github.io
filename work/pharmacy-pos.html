Pharmacy ERP / POS

Wholesale and retail in one, with offline-first billing

The problem

Pharmacies lose sales when the internet drops, and expiry dates live on batches, not products.

What I built

A design for a desktop POS on Tauri + SQLite with wholesale and retail modes, offline-first billing that syncs later, and batch-level inventory with expiry tracking.

The outcome

A clear architecture ready to build: the counter never waits for the network, and stock is accurate down to the batch.
