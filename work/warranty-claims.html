Warranty Claim System

From a customer's warranty claim to a decision, with photos and a status trail

The problem

Warranty claims arrived by phone and chat with photos scattered across devices, and there was no record of what was decided or why.

What I built

Claim creation with photo and video uploads, a status workflow with notes on every change, a claims dashboard, analytics on claim types and outcomes, and printable claim reports.

The outcome

Every claim has its evidence, its history and its decision in one place.
