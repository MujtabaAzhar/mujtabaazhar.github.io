CUVAS University Website

71 static pages migrated to a Laravel CMS, byte-identical

The problem

The university's site was 71 hand-edited HTML files. Every sitewide change meant editing all of them.

What I built

A rebuild of cuvas.edu.pk as a Laravel application with content editable at /admin. The old pages were kept as the specification, and rendered output was diffed against them through a nine-phase migration.

The outcome

All 71 pages render byte-identical to the originals, 4,151 internal links resolve, 67 tests with 708 assertions pass, and content is now edited in a browser.

Laravel · Blade · CMS admin · 67 tests · cPanel deploy
