TechSpot Video Downloader

Paste a link, pick a quality, get the file

The problem

Downloader sites are full of ads and depend on third-party APIs that break without warning.

What I built

A Laravel 12 app for public videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, with no accounts, no queue workers and no external API. yt-dlp and ffmpeg are bundled, fonts and assets self-hosted, and Linux and Windows hosting builds are scripted.

The outcome

If a CDN, registry or Google Fonts goes down, the site keeps working: the only outside servers it talks to are the video platforms themselves.

Laravel 12 · yt-dlp · ffmpeg · Vite · Self-hosted
