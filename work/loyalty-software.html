Loyalty Software

QR loyalty engine running live in Saudi car wash branches

The problem

Car washes in Makkah had no way to recognise repeat customers. Paper punch cards were lost, faked and impossible to track across branches.

What I built

A loyalty programme built around QR cards. Supervisors scan a card in seconds; the system logs the visit, sends the customer an instant WhatsApp alert and tracks progress toward rewards: a free wash after 5 visits and on birthdays.

The outcome

Running daily in real branches in Makkah. Customers get WhatsApp confirmation on every visit, and owners see who comes back and how often.

Laravel · Node.js · MySQL · WhatsApp API · QR codes
