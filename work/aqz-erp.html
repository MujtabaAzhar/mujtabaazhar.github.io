AQZ Contractors Financial ERP

Financial ERP for a contracting company, built on a mandatory double-entry ledger

The problem

A contractor's money moved through projects, suppliers, staff and petty cash, and balances drifted because they were edited directly.

What I built

A phased Laravel 12 build with a service and repository layer, where every money mutation goes through a single LedgerService and no balance is ever updated directly. Soft deletes and created_by/updated_by on every table, permissions per module, and each phase closing with migration, model, seeder, permission, UI and test.

The outcome

Balances can always be rebuilt from the ledger, so the books reconcile by design rather than by effort.

Laravel 12 · PHP 8.3 · MySQL 8 · Double-entry ledger · Spatie Permission · DataTables · TCPDF
