WashTrack

Multi-tenant SaaS that runs car wash companies end to end

The problem

Car washes in Saudi Arabia ran on cash drawers, WhatsApp groups and paper loyalty cards. Owners with several branches had no single view of washes, cash, staff or customers.

What I built

One installation serving many car wash companies, each isolated, billed on a plan and unlocking modules by entitlement. Four separate planes: a staff app with six roles, a super-admin control plane, a corporate fleet portal and a customer portal with phone + one-time code sign-in. Inside: wash pricing by service × vehicle class, shifts and cash reconciliation, tanker records, loyalty points ledger, wash packages, coupons, a generic campaign engine, expenses with approvals, P&L, HR and payroll, VAT-compliant invoicing and a REST API for the mobile apps.

The outcome

Running in production for Speed Track. 469 automated tests guard it, including cross-tenant isolation on every tenant-owned model and loyalty balances proven equal to their ledger. One cron entry drives point expiry, billing sweeps and notifications, on plain shared hosting.

Laravel 12 · PHP 8.2 · MySQL · REST API · PWA · EN/AR · RTL · 469 tests
